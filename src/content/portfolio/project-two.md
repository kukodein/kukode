## Overview

UrbanStyle Apparel is a growing fashion brand that wanted full ownership of their customer data and checkout experience without paying excessive marketplace fees.

## The Challenge

- Transitioning from third-party marketplace reliance to an owned direct-to-consumer store.
- Integrating automated shipping rate calculations and local payment gateways.
- Ensuring the catalog loads instantaneously on mobile networks.

## The Solution

We crafted a headless storefront paired with a robust e-commerce backend, providing the flexibility of modern frontend architecture with familiar product management.

- **Instant search & filtering**: Shoppers find products in real time with zero latency.
- **One-click WhatsApp support**: Shoppers can instantly inquire about sizing or shipping status.
- **Automated fulfillment**: Orders seamlessly sync with local couriers and inventory tracking.
