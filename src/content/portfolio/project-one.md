## Overview

TechCorp needed a complete revamp of their outdated corporate website. Their existing platform was slow, difficult to update, and failed to reflect their position as an industry leader in enterprise cloud solutions.

## The Challenge

- **Slow load times**: The previous CMS had bloated scripts resulting in an average page load of over 4.5 seconds.
- **Low conversion rate**: Visitors found it difficult to understand the complex service offerings and schedule consultations.
- **Content management bottleneck**: Marketing team had to rely on engineers for even simple copy edits.

## The Solution

We built a blazing-fast, static website powered by modern web technologies and an intuitive headless Git-based CMS.

1. **Lightweight architecture**: We eliminated unnecessary dependencies, delivering sub-second page loads globally.
2. **Interactive solution finder**: Potential clients can easily navigate services tailored to their specific industry needs.
3. **Streamlined editorial workflow**: Sveltia CMS enables the marketing team to publish case studies and news directly with instant previews.

## Results & Impact

- **99/100** Google PageSpeed score on mobile and desktop.
- **+145%** increase in qualified contact inquiries within the first 60 days.
- **Zero server maintenance** overhead with modern static hosting.
